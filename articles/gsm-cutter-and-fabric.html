GSM Cutter and Fabric

23 Jun, 2026

Textile Testing

By Ariful Islam Fahim

A GSM (grams per square meter) cutter is a circular sample cutter used to cut a precisely sized fabric swatch, which is then weighed to determine the fabric's mass per unit area. This measurement is one of the most fundamental checks in textile quality control, since GSM directly affects a fabric's drape, durability, and suitability for a given garment style.
