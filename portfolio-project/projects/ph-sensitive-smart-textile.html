pH-Sensitive Smart Textile Using Natural Dye

Author
Ariful Islam Fahim
Timeline
2025
Category
Wet Processing II Lab Project
Role
Researcher

Developed a cotton-based chromatic fabric using anthocyanin extract from roselle with a reversible pH-sensitive color response. Mordant concentration and thermal processing were optimized to enhance wash-fastness performance.
