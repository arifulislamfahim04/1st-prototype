This final-year engineering design project focuses on the valorization and repurposing of harmful textile sludge to mitigate environmental impacts. It is a core part of the B.Sc. in Textile Sciences and Engineering program at Green University of Bangladesh.

Full write-up, methodology, and results are in progress and will be added as the project develops.
